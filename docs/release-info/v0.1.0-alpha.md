# Release Notes: v0.1.0-alpha

**Release Date:** November 26, 2026
**Previous Version:** none (first public version)
**Codename:** Off Means Encrypted

---

## Highlights

- **No standby, ever** - Lid, power key, suspend and hibernate keys power the laptop off. There is no screen saver and no screen off that only looks like "off".
- **Instant off wipes the disk key first** - `sentinel-poweroff instant` suspends every dm-crypt mapping (`cryptsetup luksSuspend`), which removes the volume key from kernel memory, before it cuts the power. If the hardware does not power off within 15 seconds, the laptop restarts at once and stops at the passphrase prompt.
- **Settings app, stage 1** - Seven categories with working backends: Power and lock, Display and sound, Network and Tor, Devices and privacy, Keyboard and touchpad, Security, System. English by default, Deutsch as second language.
- **Optional lock screen** - Off by default; switched on only after a warning that a lock encrypts nothing.
- **Private by default** - Camera driver not loaded at boot, microphone muted at login, Bluetooth blocked at boot.

---

## Changes

### Power and lock

| Detail | Value |
|:-------|:------|
| Idle power off | 5 minutes, 15 minutes (default), 1 hour, never |
| Power button modes | Instant off (default), fast, normal, ask (power menu) |
| Battery | Power off below 3, 5 (default) or 10 % (UPower) |
| Lock screen | Optional, default off |

### Settings app layout

No titles above the content (the window title names the page), no scroll bars, no close button inside the app, sidebar with seven categories that never scrolls, content in three columns on wide screens.
